privacy

last updated nov 16, 2026

phodo is a one-pic-a-day diary you share with close friends. We built it to be the opposite of a feed that sells you. Here's exactly what we collect and why, in plain words.

what we collect

  • your email: so you can sign in with a one-time code. We never show it to other people.
  • your profile: username, display name, and anything you choose to add (bio, location, your color).
  • your posts: photos, captions, tags, the date, and whether each post is for friends or just you.
  • your connections and activity: who you're friends with, reactions and comments, boards you make, and anyone you block or report.
  • that you're 13 or older: we ask your birthday once to check this, but we only keep a yes/no, never the birthday itself.

what we don't do

  • no ads. we don't sell or rent your data, ever.
  • no tracking across other apps or websites, and no third-party analytics.
  • no location: before a photo leaves your phone, phodo removes the hidden location (GPS) and camera data from it.
  • no like or follower counts.

who can see what

  • "friends" posts are visible only to people you've accepted as friends.
  • "everyone" posts can be seen by anyone signed in to phodo, in explore. You choose this per post; the default is friends.
  • "just me" posts are visible only to you.
  • reactions on your post are visible only to you and the person who reacted.
  • boards are private to you.
  • your username, display name, bio and profile picture can be seen by other phodo users so friends can find you.
  • people you block can't see your posts, and you can't see theirs.

Photos are stored privately and are only ever shared through short-lived links that expire.

services we use

  • Supabase: stores your account, posts and photos.
  • Resend: delivers your sign-in code emails.
  • Apple: delivers notifications and app updates (TestFlight / App Store).

These services process data only to run phodo for us.

deleting your stuff

Delete any post anytime. To delete your whole account, go to profile → ☰ → delete my account. That permanently removes your profile, posts, photos, friends, reactions, comments and boards. It can't be undone.

kids

phodo is for people 13 and older. If you think someone under 13 has an account, email us and we'll remove it.

safety

Hold any post to report it or block the person. You can also email hi@phodo.app. We review reports and remove content that breaks the rules. We're required by law to report child sexual abuse material to the authorities.

changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we'll tell you in the app first.

contact

Questions, requests for a copy of your data, or anything else: hi@phodo.app
