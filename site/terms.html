phodo is a place to keep one small moment a day and share it with your people. By making an account or using phodo, you agree to these terms. They're short on purpose.

who can use phodo

  • You have to be 13 or older.
  • One person, one account. Keep your email secure, since it's how you sign in.

zero tolerance

phodo has no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Don't post or send:

  • bullying, harassment, threats, or hate toward anyone
  • nudity or sexual content, and never anything sexual involving minors
  • violence, self-harm encouragement, or anything dangerous or illegal
  • spam, scams, or impersonating someone else
  • other people's photos or private info without their permission

Hold any post to report it or block the person. We review reports, remove content that breaks these rules, and remove accounts that post it, usually within 24 hours. Child sexual abuse material is reported to the authorities.

your stuff

  • You own what you post. You give phodo permission to store it and show it to the people you chose (friends, everyone, or just you), only to run the app.
  • Only post things you have the right to share.
  • Delete any post, or your whole account, anytime in the app.

  • phodo is in early testing and is provided "as is". Things might break; we'll do our best to fix them fast.
  • We may change or remove features, and update these terms. If the change matters, we'll tell you in the app first.
  • We can suspend accounts that break these rules.
